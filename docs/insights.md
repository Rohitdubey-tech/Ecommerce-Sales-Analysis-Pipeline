# Key Business Insights (Verified Run Outputs)

1. **Delivery Delays Destroy Customer Satisfaction (`-2.50` Stars):**
   On-time or early orders (`82,100` orders) average a **4.21** review score, whereas orders delayed by `1–3 days` (`1,678` orders) drop to **3.22**, `4–7 days` (`1,537` orders) drop to **2.09**, and `8+ days` (`2,433` orders) plunge to **1.71**—proving that delivery SLA adherence is the single strongest driver of customer ratings.

2. **Black Friday Peak & Revenue Concentration:**
   Across `88,778` delivered orders (`R$ 10,853,833.63` total revenue, `R$ 122.26` AOV, `4.09` avg review), **November 2017** was the peak month with `6,723` orders and **R$ 825,312.48** in revenue—a **+61.72% MoM surge** over October 2017 (`R$ 510,333.01`).

3. **Regional Logistics Bottlenecks Outside São Paulo:**
   **São Paulo (`SP`)** generates **40.65%** of total revenue (`R$ 4,412,062.45`, `38,464` orders) with fast **8.56-day** average delivery and a **5.65%** late rate. By contrast, **Rio de Janeiro (`RJ`, `12.93%` late, `14.93` avg days)** and **Bahia (`BA`, `13.63%` late, `19.08` avg days)** suffer more than double the late-delivery rate and drop to **3.88** average review scores.

4. **Severe Repeat-Purchase Gap (`2.94%` Repeat Customers):**
   RFM K-Means segmentation across `85,967` unique delivered customers (`k=2`, silhouette `0.7127`) shows **97.06%** (`83,440` customers) are single-purchase **Hibernating** buyers (`avg_frequency = 1.00`, `avg_monetary = R$ 122.24`), while only **2.94%** (`2,527` **Champions**, `avg_frequency = 2.11`, `avg_monetary = R$ 258.76`) are repeat buyers generating **6.02%** (`R$ 653,880`) of revenue—highlighting a major retention opportunity.

5. **Category & Payment Concentration:**
   **`bed_bath_table`** leads all categories at **R$ 1,112,734.01** (`10.25%` of revenue, `8,977` orders), followed by **`health_beauty`** (`R$ 990,600.84`) and **`sports_leisure`** (`R$ 888,547.35`). **Credit cards** drive **77.50%** of revenue (`R$ 8,412,249.75`, averaging `3.43` installments), compared to **18.88%** for `boleto` (`R$ 2,049,223.05`).
